## 📈 Insights
- 📉 Significant revenue drop (~60%) in September  
- 📍 São Paulo is the top revenue-generating city  
- 💳 Credit cards dominate (~78%) transactions  
- 📦 High successful delivery rate  
- 🚚 Late deliveries around 7–8%
